# Stroke Check guidance

The agent may answer family questions only with text from this file. If nothing here matches, it says "Ask the 911 dispatcher."
This is general public guidance, not medical advice. The 911 dispatcher's instructions come first.

Sources (read on 2026-11-21):
- [CDC1] CDC, "Signs and Symptoms of Stroke": https://www.cdc.gov/stroke/signs-symptoms/index.html
- [CDC2] CDC, "Treatment and Intervention for Stroke": https://www.cdc.gov/stroke/treatment/index.html
- [CDC3] CDC, "About Stroke": https://www.cdc.gov/stroke/about/index.html
- [MED] MedlinePlus, "Stroke": https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000726.htm
- [NHS] NHS, "Symptoms of a stroke": https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/stroke/symptoms/

## be_fast
Keywords: signs, symptoms, be fast, fast, balance, eyes, vision, face, droop, smile, arm, drift, weak, speech, slurred, strange, señales, síntomas, dấu hiệu, triệu chứng
B.E. F.A.S.T. helps spot a stroke. Balance: are they off-balance or dizzy? Eyes: do they have trouble seeing? Face: ask them to smile; does one side droop? Arms: ask them to raise both arms; does one drift down? Speech: is it slurred or strange? Time: if you see any of these signs, call 911 right away.
Source: [CDC1]

## call_911
Keywords: call, 911, ambulance, emergency, help, now, should, llamar, ambulancia, emergencia, gọi, cấp cứu, xe cứu thương
Call 911 right away if someone shows signs of a stroke. Strokes need emergency care. A short episode (TIA) is a medical emergency too, and there is no way to know at the start which it is.
Source: [CDC3], [CDC1]

## symptoms_go_away
Keywords: better, gone, away, stopped, improve, improving, feels fine, wait, okay now, mejor, se fue, esperar, đỡ, khỏi, hết, chờ
Do not wait, even if the signs stop. Symptoms can stop after a short time, so the person may seem okay. Get medical help straight away anyway. Call 911.
Source: [NHS], [CDC3]

## note_the_time
Keywords: time, when, started, last, normal, clock, timer, why, hora, cuándo, tiempo, giờ, lúc, thời gian, khi nào
Note the time when the symptoms first appeared, or when the person was last seen acting normal. This helps doctors choose the best treatment. The treatments that work best must start within a few hours of the first symptoms.
Source: [CDC1], [CDC2], [MED]

## do_not_drive
Keywords: drive, car, take him, take her, hospital, myself, ourselves, faster, conducir, manejar, llevar, carro, coche, lái xe, chở, tự đưa, bệnh viện
Do not drive to the hospital, and do not let someone else drive them. Call 911 for an ambulance. Paramedics can start life-saving treatment on the way and alert the hospital before you arrive, so the person may be treated more quickly.
Source: [CDC2], [NHS]

## other_symptoms
Keywords: numb, numbness, confused, confusion, headache, dizzy, walk, walking, see, seeing, vision, understand, entumecido, confusión, dolor de cabeza, mareo, tê, lú lẫn, đau đầu, chóng mặt
Other sudden signs: numbness or weakness in the face, arm or leg, especially on one side; confusion or trouble speaking or understanding; trouble seeing; trouble walking, dizziness or loss of balance; a sudden severe headache with no known cause. Tell the 911 dispatcher about any of these.
Source: [CDC1]

## hospital_questions
Keywords: paramedics, hospital, doctor, ask, information, history, need, know, paramédicos, médico, nhân viên cấp cứu, bác sĩ, hỏi
At the hospital, staff will ask about the person's medical history and the time the symptoms started. Paramedics also collect this information on the way.
Source: [CDC2]

## food_drink_medicine
Keywords: aspirin, medicine, medication, pill, tablet, give, eat, food, drink, water, insulin, aspirina, medicina, pastilla, comida, agua, beber, thuốc, aspirin, ăn, uống, nước
Do not give any medicine, food or drink, including aspirin. Only the paramedics and doctors decide on medicine. Ask the 911 dispatcher if you are unsure.
Source: [TEAM]

## while_waiting
Keywords: wait, waiting, meanwhile, until, arrive, do now, what now, door, list, medications, stay, alone, mientras, esperar, puerta, lista, quedarse, trong lúc chờ, cửa, danh sách, ở lại
While you wait: stay with the person, follow the 911 dispatcher's instructions, unlock the door for the paramedics, and gather a list of their current medications to hand over.
Source: [TEAM]
